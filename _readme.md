# Timeland
A GPS track ridden through time, with photos surfacing where they were taken.

**Date**: 2004, rebuilt 2026
**For**: personal, made with Even Westvang
**Contains**: the original 2004 Flash piece and a 2026 rebuild in plain HTML and Canvas. `index.html` is the running 2026 version, live at timoarnall.github.io/timeland-2004. `data/` holds the GPS track, photo EXIF index, and the 241 source JPEGs from a 1,500 km Iceland trip, 30 June to 6 July 2004. `_archive_2004/` keeps the original Flash file, its source, a screen recording, and a decompilation. `_speculation_other_datasets/` and `experiment-2026/` hold rebuild experiments. `_Documentation/` has the day-by-day rebuild notes and comparison captures.

See README.md for the full story, including the two display bugs in the original Flash that the rebuild corrects (a two-hour clock offset, and a longitude reversal).
